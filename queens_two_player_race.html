How to Race

  • 1 Place exactly one Queen in each row, column, and colored region on the 7×7 grid.
  • 2 Queens cannot touch each other, even diagonally.
  • 3 Player 1 (Left): Move with W, A, S, D and press F to toggle a Queen.
  • 4 Player 2 (Right): Move with Arrow Keys and press Enter to toggle a Queen. First to solve wins the round's kitty!
  • P.S. Press 1 (Player 1) or 0 (Player 2) anytime to make your won kitties jump for joy!
